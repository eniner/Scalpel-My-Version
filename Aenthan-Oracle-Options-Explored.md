# Aenthan — Oracle options explored (not just “keep totems”)

You asked why Spark / Cast-on-Crit Comet wouldn’t just be better. Fair question. Below is the honest comparison **against your live kit**, using Oracle mechanics from the ascendancy docs + current 0.5 build guides — not a single-path sales pitch.

Live kit anchors: **CI · Ancestral Bond · Forced Outcome · Unnamed Heartwood · Mageblood · Whispering Ice · Victory Call (spirit sceptre) · Corpse Bane (+5 cold) · ~406 Spirit · ~11k ES · Grim Pillars totem 6-link · Entangle detonator**.

## 1. Oracle — what actually matters (Fextralife is incomplete)

Fextralife’s [Oracle](https://pathofexile2.wiki.fextralife.com/Oracle) page is a stub (flavor + “staves/talismans”). The useful kit is:

| Notable | What it does for *any* crit caster |
|---------|-------------------------------------|
| **The Unseen Path** | Reveals Oracle-only “Paths Not Taken” nodes (totem clusters, movement, damage nodes others can’t see). You already use this. |
| **Entwined Realities** | Allocate notables near keystones without a connected path — tree efficiency / off-path grabs. |
| **Forced Outcome** | Rerolls until you crit (crit multi penalty while rerolling). Enables **100% crit uptime** → god-tier for **Cast on Critical energy** *and* for crit-scaled totem hits. |
| **The Lesser Harm** | Enemy hits/crits Unlucky — your main Oracle defensive. |
| **Unnamed Heartwood** | +1 totem + totems linger after death — **totem-specific**. Wasted on pure CoC. |
| **Converging Paths / Fateful Vision** | Moment of Vulnerability / Align Fate — optional micro-DPS windows; neither requires totems. |

**Takeaway:** Forced Outcome does **not** force you into totems. It forces you into *something that crits a lot*. Totems, Spark, Frostbolt, and CoC Comet all abuse it. Ascendancy alone does not pick the winner — **your gear + keystones do**.

## 2. Contenders on *your* gear

### A) Grim Pillars Spell Totem + Entangle shatter (what you play)
**How it wins:** Spell Totem bypasses Grim Pillars’ Runic Ward cost. Pillars spawn ice crystals; Entangle + Mórrigan/Biting Frost **shatters** them for the real clear/boss nuke. Bond doubles totem count; Heartwood keeps them up; sceptre spirit pays Bond’s 75 spirit/totem.

**Fits your gear:** Almost perfectly (Bond, Heartwood, Victory Call, cold wand, Whispering Ice as swap, Mageblood).

**Weakness:** Two-button (totems + Entangle), pillars can clutter, lineage gems (Vorana/Rakiata) are investment.

### B) Spark Spell Totem (swap Pillars → Spark, keep Bond)
**How it wins:** One-button clear, projectiles fill screen, no crystal detonator. Same Ancestral Bond / Heartwood / sceptre chassis. Guides (Allie and others) even run **Spark + Grim Pillars on the same totem setup** as progression.

**On your gear:** Drop-in **skill** swap. You keep tree, belt, sceptre, Bond. You **lose** the crystal-shatter multiplier that makes Pillars S-tier boss/chunk. Cold-specific supports (Rakiata, Cold Mastery focus) become less optimal — you’d retune toward lightning/proj/chain.

**When Spark totems beat Pillars for you:** If you hate detonating, want smoother mapping, or bosses already die before Entangle matters.  
**When they lose:** Single-target and “screen explode” ceilings — Pillars’ shatter is why the meta parked here.

### C) Comet Spell Totem
Guides note Comet often has **highest raw totem hit** but **slow cast + ground delay** feels worse for clear. Same chassis as A/B. With your cold wand/staff, Comet is plausible on bosses as a **socket swap**, not a full rebrand.

### D) Self-cast Spark (or Frostbolt) + Cast on Critical → Comet
**How it wins:** Forced Outcome → every hit crits → CoC fills energy constantly → Comets rain. Maxroll Frostbolt CoC wants **Snakepit** (fork, not pierce) + Frost Wall crystal chains. Mobalytics Spark CoC variants often want **Archmage + huge spirit reservation**, and some top sheets use **Covenant + life costs + leech** (anti-CI).

**What you’d have to abandon on Aenthan:**

| You have | CoC Spark/Comet usually wants |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Ancestral Bond | **No** — spirit eaten by totems, tree invested in placement |
| Unnamed Heartwood | **Dead notable** |
| Victory Call spirit sceptre | Often **Focus / wand+focus**, Archmage spirit package |
| Totem placement / Supportive Ancestors / etc. | Full **respec** into caster CoC / ailment / proj tree |
| CI + ES jewels (recharge start, ES%) | Some CoC sheets are **life + Covenant + MoM mana pool** — opposite |
| Entangle detonator kit | Irrelevant |
| Whispering Ice | Usable for cold CoC, but not the enabler |

**Spirit math:** CoC + Archmage style setups often want ~200+ reserved spirit. Your 406 spirit is currently paying **Bond totems**. Free the spirit → CoC becomes possible — but only after ripping Bond and the totem half of the tree.

**Snakepit:** Frostbolt CoC’s build-enabling unique. You don’t have it. Without it, Maxroll says don’t even play that Frostbolt pattern.

### E) Hybrid: keep Bond totems, add CoC on the side
Tempting because Forced Outcome feeds CoC. In practice: **spirit double-dips** (Bond totems + CoC reservation), and your main hits are **totem hits** — CoC typically needs **you** to crit. Totem crits usually **do not** charge *your* Cast on Critical the way self-casts do (treat as unreliable unless you’ve verified in-game). So “totems + CoC Comet” is usually a trap unless you’re also self-casting a crit spam skill.

## 3. Direct answer: “Why wouldn’t Spark/CoC Comet be better?”

**Spark/CoC Comet can be better** — on a character built for it (Forced Outcome + selfcast projectile engine + CoC spirit + usually Snakepit or Covenant/Archmage package).

**On Aenthan right now, it is not better**, because:

1. **Your keystone is Ancestral Bond.** That’s a totem build declaration. CoC’s best sheets don’t run it.
2. **Your ascendancy 4th big node is Heartwood** — totem life. CoC gets zero.
3. **Your offhand is a spirit sceptre** — Bond fuel. CoC wants that spirit for meta gems instead.
4. **Your 6-link and lineage gems** are cold-pillar / totem placement. Respec cost isn’t just passives — it’s gem links and play muscle memory.
5. **CI + ES recharge jewels** match Pillars/totem ES mapping, not Covenant-leech CoC.
6. Forced Outcome is already fully used: your Grim Pillars hits are 100% crit @ 458% multi. CoC doesn’t uniquely “unlock” Forced Outcome for you — you already spent that card.

So: CoC isn’t “smarter Oracle play.” It’s a **different gear thesis** that throws away the expensive parts of *this* character.

## 6. Practical recommendation

1. Stay **Grim Pillars Ancestral Bond Oracle** on this gear.  
2. Explore **Spark totems** as a map-clear alternate socket, not a full CoC pivot.  
3. Revisit **CoC Comet** only if you’re willing to: refund Bond + Heartwood + totem clusters, replace sceptre role, and buy the CoC enablers (Snakepit and/or Archmage spirit package / non-CI mana solution).

Oracle’s foresight kit (Forced Outcome + Unseen Path) supports **both** worlds. Your **items** already voted for totems.
