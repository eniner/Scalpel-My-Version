## Cold rotation (ultimate)

**Maps**
1. Totems down (Pillars paint crystals)  
2. Entangle through packs (shatter)  
3. Mana Tempest on CD  
4. Swap Whispering Ice → Icestorm into already-chilled/frozen packs for upgraded bolts  
5. Convalescence when ES is suppressed  

**Bosses / tanky rares**
1. Hypothermia / Elemental Weakness  
2. Heart of Ice or Frost Bomb (exposure)  
3. Frost Wall on/around the boss  
4. Full totem battery  
5. Entangle on CD to shatter Wall + Pillars  
6. Whispering Ice Icestorm once freeze is up (consume → improved bolts)  
7. Repeat Wall as charges allow
